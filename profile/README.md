# 💫 About Me:
**About CLP Studio**<br><br>CLP Studio is a technology-driven agency building full-stack applications, modern websites, AI voice agents, AI chatbots, and intelligent automation solutions at affordable prices without compromising on security.<br><br>Beyond client projects, we actively develop useful digital tools and products, with a focus on making technology accessible, practical, and user-friendly for individuals and organizations. We have already built several free tools and are working to open-source more of our projects, encouraging transparency, collaboration, and community-driven development.<br><br>Our goal is simple: build secure, reliable, and accessible technology that solves real-world problems while helping businesses and communities grow.
